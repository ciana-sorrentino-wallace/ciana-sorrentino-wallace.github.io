COVID-19 Executive Orders Project

Student researcher with Dr. Peter Federman (IU O’Neill) and Dr. Cali Curley (University of Miami), tracking and coding more than 1,500 COVID-19 executive orders from every state into a public dashboard that compares the stringency of state responses. About

Research that people can actually use.

For four years I was a Senior Operations and Research Analyst at the Indiana Department of Child Services, where I led multi-state policy research, evaluated programs, investigated data integrity problems and supported federal reporting and audits. I was the agency's designated data champion for validating AFCARS reports during a statewide case management system consolidation.

Since then I’ve done independent policy analysis and program evaluation, including a peer-region housing policy study.

I care about the space between policy on paper and practice on the ground: how a rule reads to a caseworker, whether a program reaches the families it was built for, and what the data really shows.
